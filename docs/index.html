AutoTiktok is a personal, non-commercial tool. It publishes short videos created by its owner to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts, using each platform's official API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy Policy

Data accessed. With the account owner's explicit authorization (OAuth), AutoTiktok accesses only what is needed to publish videos:

  • TikTok: basic profile information (user.info.basic) and the permission to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish).
  • Instagram: basic account information and content publishing permission.
  • YouTube: the permission to upload videos (youtube.upload).

Storage. Access tokens are stored encrypted, and are used only to publish the owner's videos. AutoTiktok keeps a log of published videos (file name, post identifier, date). It does not collect data about other users, viewers or followers.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Data is only sent to the platform it belongs to (TikTok, Meta, Google) when publishing.

Deletion. Access can be revoked at any time from the settings of each platform. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; they are also deleted on request at CONTACT_EMAIL.
